## ⚠️ LinkedIn Safety Limits

**To avoid account restrictions:**
- Max 100 connection requests/day (safer: 30-50)
- Max 150 DMs/day (safer: 50-75)
- Don't copy-paste identical messages (vary each one)
- Mix engagement: likes + comments + shares
- Take breaks between actions (5-10 min)

---

## 📈 Optimization Loop (Daily)

1. **Track which comments get engagement** → do more of those
2. **Track which connection requests get accepted** → refine targeting
3. **Track which DMs get replies** → refine messaging
4. **Track which posts get views** → create more of that content
5. **Track which leads convert to demos** → focus on those industries

---

## 🚨 Red Flags to Avoid

❌ Don't pitch immediately in connection request
❌ Don't spam identical messages
❌ Don't send more than 100 requests/day
❌ Don't ignore people who reply
❌ Don't give up after 1 week (takes 2-4 weeks for momentum)

---

## ✅ Quick Wins to Accelerate

1. **Post before connecting** - they see your content in feed
2. **Comment on their posts first** - warm up the connection
3. **Mention mutual connections** - increases acceptance rate
4. **Use their name** - personalization matters
5. **Keep messages under 100 words** - respect their time
