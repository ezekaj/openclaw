## How to Add Images to Your Ad

### Method 1: During Ad Creation/Editing
1. Go to the "Bilder (empfohlen)" section
2. Click **"Bilder hinzufügen"** button
3. A file dialog will open
4. Select your image file (supported formats: JPG, PNG, GIF)
5. You can upload up to 20 images (max 12 MB each)
6. Drag images to reorder them
7. First image will be the main thumbnail

### Method 2: Edit Existing Ad
1. Go to your ad page
2. Click **"Bearbeiten"** (Edit) button
3. Scroll to "Bilder (empfohlen)" section
4. Click **"Bilder hinzufügen"**
5. Upload your images
6. Click **"Anzeige speichern"** to save changes

### Image Best Practices
- **High quality:** Use clear, well-lit photos
- **Professional:** Show your work portfolio screenshots
- **Variety:** Include different project examples
- **Size:** Optimize images (under 12 MB each)
- **First image:** Make it your best/most attractive work

## Tips for Better Ads

### 1. Use Images Effectively
- Add screenshots of your best websites
- Show before/after redesigns
- Include your logo/branding
- Add team photos (professional)

### 2. Highlight Your Portfolio
- Make URLs clickable (copy-paste friendly)
- Brief description of each project
- Mention technologies used
- Client testimonials if available

### 3. Pricing Strategy
- Use "VB" (Verhandlungsbasis) for flexibility
- Show starting price ("ab 499 EUR")
- Offer package deals
- Be transparent about what's included

### 4. Local SEO
- Mention "Ulm" multiple times naturally
- Include nearby areas: "Ulm und Umgebung"
- Add your PLZ (89073)
- Mention "vor Ort" (on-site) service

### 5. Contact Information
- Encourage direct contact
- Offer free consultation
- Respond quickly to inquiries
- Provide multiple contact methods
